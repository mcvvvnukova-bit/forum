# External identification without registration moderation

Participant accounts are created only after successful external identification: individuals through Sber ID, and legal entities or individual entrepreneurs through Kontur.Diadok. The platform does not create a registration application, verification status, or manual Moderator decision because an additional internal approval would duplicate the trusted external identity step, slow onboarding, and introduce a state that no product workflow needs. Failed identification creates no account; successful identification grants the participant its role immediately. Content moderation remains mandatory for Lots, reviews, and review responses. This decision supersedes ADR-0001.
